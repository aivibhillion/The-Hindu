Centre mulls a Special Session of Parliament to pass the Delimitation Bill, 2026

GS-II PolityParliament · Constitution amendment · Women’s reservationDelimitation · Women’s Reservation Act · Special majority · ProrogationMains

Government sources said the Centre is considering a Special Session to pass the Delimitation Bill, 2026, which it links to operationalising the Women’s Reservation Act, 2023. The proposed Constitution amendment would raise Lok Sabha strength from 543 to a maximum of 850, based on the 2011 Census, enabling the 33% quota in Parliament and State Assemblies. The Centre needs 361 votes for a two-thirds majority (the House of 543 has three vacancies) and says it has “at least 15–16 more votes than needed” (a government claim; dates and duration not disclosed). A similar amendment failed in the Lok Sabha on 17 April. The Monsoon Session, adjourned sine die on 13 August, has not been prorogued for 91 days, breaking the 87-day record of 2008. In April the Centre told South Indian parties it would raise seats for all States by 50% to preserve inter-State ratios based on 1971 figures. Congress’s Jairam Ramesh said the plan “will not work”. Claims on both sides are political and not verified.

📌 Static GK

Seat allocation among States was frozen on the 1971 Census (42nd Amendment, 1976), extended to the first Census after 2026 (84th Amendment, 2001). The 106th Amendment (Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam, 2023) reserves one-third of seats for women. A Constitution amendment needs a majority of total membership and two-thirds of those present and voting (Article 368).

UPSC 🎯 Prelims pointers

  • Article 368; 84th Amendment; 106th Amendment.

APPSC / TGPSC 🏛️ Exam angle

  • LS 543 to 850; 361 votes needed.

Explainer: why the Siang Upper Multipurpose Project is contentious

GS-III InfrastructureHydropower · Arunachal Pradesh · Water securitySUMP · NHPC · Pre-Feasibility Report · Adi communityMains

The proposed Siang Upper Multipurpose Project (SUMP) in Arunachal Pradesh, to be built by NHPC on the Siang (the Brahmaputra’s name there), was declared a national project in 2008, with a capacity of 11,000–11,200 MW. Deputy Chief Minister Chowna Mein cites a dam height of 280–300 m and about 9.2 billion cubic metres of live storage and says it would regulate water, cut flood risk and create jobs. The Adi community says at least 40 villages would be affected and 27 submerged (their claim); protests and detentions have continued since October, and groups rejected talks on 5 November. The project is only at the Pre-Feasibility Report stage; full impact study (a year of baseline data), dam-break analysis and a disaster plan are still needed. Mein argues SUMP could cushion sudden upstream releases from China’s Medog dam. Note: earlier digests gave different capacity figures (11,000 versus 11,200 MW); both appear in official statements.

📌 Static GK

The Brahmaputra is called Siang/Dihang in Arunachal, then Brahmaputra in Assam. Live storage is the usable volume; the India–China treaty gap on river data sharing is a continuing concern.

UPSC 🎯 Prelims pointers

  • Siang/Dihang; live storage.

APPSC / TGPSC 🏛️ Exam angle

  • SUMP 11,000–11,200 MW.

CII Summit Day 1: 73 MoUs and 15 G.O.s worth ₹5.84 lakh crore

GS-III EconomyInvestment · IndustryMoU · G.O. · Renewable energy · Kia expansionPrelims

On Day 1 of the 31st Confederation of Indian Industry (CII) Partnership Summit, 73 MoUs were signed and 15 Government Orders issued for commitments of ₹5,84,355 crore with potential for 1,07,693 jobs. The largest: Reliance Industries ₹2.73 lakh crore (East Godavari), Greenko ₹45,000 crore, CleanMax ₹41,000 crore, Megha Engineering ₹36,000 crore, Kia India ₹18,878 crore (output up by two lakh cars, past six lakh capacity), Rashmi Metallurgical ₹18,500 crore, and JSW and Waaree ₹15,000 crore each. General manufacturing led with 23 MoUs and 5 G.O.s (₹3,57,386 crore); renewable energy had 7 MoUs and 5 G.O.s (₹1,65,020 crore). Separately Kia and suppliers plan about $2 billion for a second plant, production from early 2030, about 12,000 direct jobs. Conflicts with 12 Nov: that day’s plan was 141 MoUs worth ₹12.14 lakh crore, but the Chief Minister said 303 MoUs worth ₹9.89 lakh crore would be signed, which lifts proposals since June 2024 to ₹32.49 lakh crore (22.6 + 9.89, derived); the Day-1 total therefore is not directly comparable. One report puts CleanMax at ₹41,000 crore and another, quoting Minister Lokesh, at ₹36,000 crore.

Twin cyclones: Konaseema and West Godavari count losses; Central team begins a tour

GS-III Disaster managementCyclones · LivelihoodsVery Severe Cyclonic Storm · Compensation · Crop insuranceMains

A Hindu report says two cyclones hit between 28 October and 3 November: Very Severe Cyclonic Storm Arnab (gales of about 150 kmph on 28–29 October) and Severe Cyclonic Storm Murasu (landfall near Ongole, heavy rain on 3–4 November). In Konaseema, over 73,500 coconut trees were uprooted and paddy destroyed on 1,18,109 acres, leaving about 88,000 farmers affected; in West Godavari 27,397 coconut trees fell, affecting over 5,000 growers. The Chief Minister put economic losses at about ₹6,500 crore (approximate; a State report is pending), with at least 20 deaths. Compensation is ₹1,500 per tree, which growers call inadequate; district officials say none opted for crop insurance. A seven-member Inter-Ministerial Central Team led by MHA Joint Secretary Chittaranjan Das will tour Bapatla and Eluru on Friday, and Palnadu and East Godavari on Saturday. Update on 11 Nov: Krishna district paddy damage was 13.21%.

📌 Static GK

The NDMA and State Disaster Management Authorities work under the Disaster Management Act, 2005. Relief is met from the State Disaster Response Force (SDRF)/National Disaster Response Force (NDRF); an IMCT assesses damage before central assistance.

Chennai Metro microbes

Study: swabbing Chennai Metro surfaces reveals a distinct “transit biome”

GS-III Science and TechnologyMicrobiology · Antimicrobial resistanceMicrobiome · AMR genes · MetagenomicsPrelims

IIT-Madras researchers swabbed 96 samples from 12 stations and trains of the Chennai Metro’s Blue and Green Lines in December 2021 and January 2022, on four high-touch surfaces: station handrails, ticket kiosks, train rods and ticket counters. They found a distinct Chennai-specific microbial community (nine “unique-core” species), with kiosks the most diverse surface, and annotated 206 antimicrobial-resistance (AMR) gene families across 25 drug classes, with sulfonamide and rifamycin resistance genes most abundant. Researchers said there is no immediate cause for fear; sampling was during the COVID-19 period of regular sanitisation, which caused many samples to fail quality control. The paper appeared in mSphere.

📌 Static GK

AMR arises when microbes resist drugs; India’s National Action Plan on AMR (2017) addressed it. A microbiome is the community of microbes in an environment.
