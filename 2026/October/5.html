The editorial pages: is the SIR backed by law? P.D.T. Achary’s argument

GS-II PolityConstitution · RP Act 1950Section 21 · Article 324 · Article 326Mains

Former Lok Sabha Secretary General P.D.T. Achary argues that the Election Commission of India (ECI)’s conduct “raises serious questions about legality, accountability and constitutional propriety”.

  • Constituent Assembly: members including Ambedkar agreed the election machinery should be outside executive control; Ambedkar centralised it after provincial CECs, mostly political nominees, had deleted opposition voters.
  • The law: Section 21 of the Representation of the People Act, 1950 allows intensive or summary revision (Rule 25 of the 1960 Rules) and a “special revision” for any constituency or part; the author says there is no “special intensive revision” in law and Article 324 cannot override statute.
  • Decision-making: two Election Commissioners have complained they were not kept in the loop; the author says a unilateral Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) decision is not valid.
  • Safeguards: a CEC can be impeached like a judge, but presiding officers can reject a motion at the threshold, and Section 16 of the 2023 Act gives protection from legal proceedings for acts done in office. These are the author’s views.

📌 Static GK

The Constituent Assembly debated the Election Commission as Draft Article 289; it is Article 324 in the Constitution. Rule 25 of the Registration of Electors Rules, 1960 covers revision.

UPSC 🎯 Prelims pointers

  • Draft Article 289 → Article 324.
  • Representation of the People Act (RP Act) 1950 – electoral rolls; RP Act 1951 – conduct of elections.

APPSC / TGPSC 🏛️ Exam angle

  • Opposition parties planning impeachment motion against CEC.

REITs and fractional real estate: how they differ

GS-III EconomyCapital markets · REITsREIT · SEBI · SPVPrelims

The Moneywise page explains how investors can get real estate exposure without buying a flat.

  • REIT rules: a REIT is a trust; it must keep at least 80% of assets in completed, rent-earning property and distribute at least 90% of net distributable cash flow (also at the SPV level). Payouts mix interest, dividend, rent and loan repayment, each taxed differently.
  • Risks: units trade like shares and can fall; rent is not guaranteed. Since January, mutual funds treat REIT units as equity.
  • Fractional ownership: one property, one address; a single vacancy hits hard and unlisted set-ups have no exchange to sell on.

📌 Static GK

REIT = Real Estate Investment Trust, regulated by SEBI (REIT Regulations, 2014). InvIT is the infrastructure equivalent. Indian REITs are mostly commercial because home rental yields are thin.

Rabies: official data shows tens of deaths, an ICMR study estimates 5,726 a year

GS-II HealthPublic health · ZoonosesICMR · IDSP-IHIP · MCCDPrelims

Government sources count a tiny fraction of the deaths a modelled ICMR estimate suggests.

  • Sources: the Health Ministry’s surveillance programme (IDSP-IHIP) reported about 50 to 54 deaths (the headline gives 50 for 2023; the text gives 54 for 2024); the MCCD system (medically certified causes of death) recorded 66 deaths in 2022 and 271 in 2023 but covers only about 22% of registered deaths; the ICMR estimate is 5,726 a year, from a survey of 337,808 people in 78,807 households in 60 districts of 15 States.
  • Estimates: about 9.1 million animal bites a year, including 5.6 million dog bites; WHO estimates about 59,000 rabies deaths worldwide, with India accounting for 35%.
  • Goal: India plans to eliminate dog-mediated human rabies by 2030. A Health Ministry official said the figures are generated through different methods and are not directly equivalent.
🎬 Rabies deaths: three data sources
Different methods; not directly comparable.

📌 Static GK
