Nobel Prize in Physics 2026: Francis Halzen for the IceCube neutrino observatory

GS-III SciencePhysics · Nobel PrizesIceCube · Neutrino · Cherenkov lightPrelims

The Nobel Prize in Physics 2026 went to Francis Halzen (82, Belgium-born, University of Wisconsin–Madison) for IceCube, a detector in Antarctic ice that spots high-energy cosmic neutrinos.

  • Detector: one cubic kilometre of ice (about 917 million litres) with 5,160 optical sensors, completed in 2010. A neutrino rarely interacts; when it does, the charged particle produces Cherenkov light, which the sensors record.
  • Findings: first high-energy cosmic neutrinos reported in 2013; traced to a blazar in 2018. Nobel committee chair: Mark Pearce.
🎬 IceCube timeline

📌 Static GK

Neutrinos are nearly massless, electrically neutral particles. A blazar is an active galaxy whose jet points toward Earth. India’s INO (India-based Neutrino Observatory) is a proposed project in Theni, Tamil Nadu.

UPSC 🎯 Prelims pointers

  • IceCube – South Pole; neutrinos.

APPSC / TGPSC 🏛️ Exam angle

  • Cherenkov radiation – faster than light in a medium.

Why NASA wants a nuclear reactor near the Moon’s south pole by 2030

GS-III ScienceSpace · Lunar missionsFission reactor · ILRS · ShackletonPrelims

NASA plans a 20 kilowatt (kW) reactor near the lunar south pole by 2030; Russia’s planned reactor for the China-led International Lunar Research Station (ILRS) would give about 10 kW.

  • Why nuclear: most of the Moon has a fortnight of daylight then a fortnight of darkness, and some craters fall below –200 degrees Celsius; a reactor works in the dark.
  • Water ice: splitting it gives hydrogen and oxygen; a 2020 NASA assessment put producing 10 tonnes of oxygen at about 68 kW of power.

Harmanpreet Kaur stepped down as captain across all three formats after a tenure that peaked with India’s maiden women’s One Day International (ODI) World Cup title last year. Smriti Mandhana was named successor; the announcement followed a meeting with Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) secretary Devajit Saikia and head coach Amol Muzumdar in Guwahati.

India won the 2025 Women’s ODI World Cup. The BCCI is the Board of Control for Cricket in India.

Football: India lose 1-6 to Uruguay in Kolkata

SportsFootball · FriendlyUruguay · Darwin Nunez · Anirudh ThapaPrelims

Uruguay’s scorers: Darwin Nunez (11, penalty), Maxi Araujo (28, 59), Rodrigo Zalazar (53), Juan Sanabria (71) and Rodrigo Aguirre (86). Coach Diego Forlan made seven changes at the break.
