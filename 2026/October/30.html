Supreme Court asks the RBI to make banks indemnify “digital arrest” victims

GS-III Internal SecurityCyber fraud · Banking regulationDigital arrest · KYC Directions · IndemnityMains

A Bench headed by CJI Surya Kant called upon the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) to require financial institutions to fully indemnify digital arrest victims where the crime resulted from a lack of institutional diligence. The court noted that over ₹800 crore reached fraudsters impersonating law enforcement in a year and said it wanted to examine the proposed Reserve Bank of India (RBI) (Know Your Customer) Amendment Directions, 2026. It said banks must be able to suspend transactions that deviate sharply from an account’s history, relay automated alerts to the police, and follow up with physical verification. States were asked for data on First Information Reports (FIRs), investigations and prosecutions; the case returns before 30 November.

  • Government position: Attorney-General R. Venkataramani said the Directions would be notified by 30 November and operationalised by 31 December, and advised against haste.
  • Court’s remarks: it called digital arrest the “biggest financial fraud in the history of this country” and said “bank employees are involved”. These are oral remarks, not findings.

📌 Static GK

“Digital arrest” is not a legal term: no law allows arrest over a phone or video call. KYC norms flow from the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 and RBI directions. An amicus curiae is a friend of the court; here it is N.S. Nappinai.

UPSC 🎯 Prelims pointers

  • KYC; Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA) 2002; indemnity.

APPSC / TGPSC 🏛️ Exam angle

  • Amicus curiae – N.S. Nappinai.

Supreme Court: orders on Deepavali firecrackers breached “left and right”

GS-III EnvironmentPollution · Fundamental rightsArticle 25 · Green crackers · EnforcementMains

A Bench of Justices M.M. Sundresh and P.B. Varale said that year after year its orders to curb firecrackers had been violated. It said religious freedom is subject to public health and other fundamental rights, but it was not in favour of a complete ban, warning of a “spiralling effect”. It asked the Centre and Delhi government, through Additional Solicitor-General Aishwarya Bhati, to enforce rules against sellers of spurious and banned crackers, and floated the idea of using celebrities such as Virat Kohli for awareness. In October 2025 it had allowed green crackers in Delhi-NCR during Deepavali, in fixed hours. Orders will be pronounced in a couple of days.

📌 Static GK

Article 25 guarantees freedom of religion, subject to public order, morality and health. The right to a clean environment is read into Article 21. Green crackers are formulations that emit less particulate matter.

UPSC 🎯 Prelims pointers

  • Article 25; Article 21; green crackers.

APPSC / TGPSC 🏛️ Exam angle

Census 2027: trainers brace for tough questions before 1 December enumeration

GS-II GovernanceCensus · Data collectionPopulation enumeration · Caste column · Aadhaar optionalPrelims

Hundreds of training sessions are under way in Uttar Pradesh and three other poll-bound States before door-to-door population enumeration begins on 1 December, the second phase of Census 2027. The rest of the country follows in February 2027. This phase has 40 questions, 13 of them new, and each respondent takes at least 20–25 minutes; an enumeration block has 750–800 people. Caste (other than Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes) is typed in by hand with no separate Other Backward Classes (OBC) column. A trainer was reported telling enumerators Aadhaar data could help as future “proof of citizenship”; a senior Census official said no such instruction was passed and the Aadhaar question is optional.

📌 Static GK

The Census Act, 1948 governs the census; Census is entry 69 of the Union List. The first phase, houselisting, collected housing data; the second counts people. Government has said Aadhaar is not proof of citizenship.

UPSC 🎯 Prelims pointers

  • Census Act 1948; Union List entry 69.

APPSC / TGPSC 🏛️ Exam angle

  • Enumeration begins 1 December 2026.

Supreme Court strikes down Karnataka law validating a “corrupt” 2011 recruitment

GS-II PolityJudiciary · Separation of powersValidation Act · 2011 KPSC recruitment · Age relaxationMains

A Bench of Justices J.B. Pardiwala and K. Vinod Chandran declared unconstitutional the Karnataka Civil Services (Validation of Selection and Appointment of 2011 Batch Gazetted Probationers) Act, 2022. It said the law was enacted to countermand judgments of the High Court and the Supreme Court that upheld the cancellation of the 2011 recruitment for 362 Group A and B posts. The process, run by the State Public Service Commission, drew 1,38,307 applicants; a Criminal Investigation Department inquiry found “gross manipulations”. Justice Chandran said the legislature cannot nullify judgments. The court directed fresh recruitment for the vacant posts, with one-time age relaxation for untainted appointees, six appellants and the petitioners before the tribunal.

📌 Static GK

A legislature can remove the legal basis of a judgment by a valid law but cannot simply overrule it; separation of powers is part of the basic structure. Public Service Commissions are constitutional bodies under Article 315.

UPSC 🎯 Prelims pointers

  • Separation of powers; Article 315.

APPSC / TGPSC 🏛️ Exam angle

  • Validation Act 2022 struck down.

Cyclone Arnab: Andhra Pradesh puts loss at ₹4,600 crore; Centre’s team arrives Friday

GS-III Disaster ManagementCyclone · Damage assessmentPreliminary loss · Central team · CompensationPrelims

Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu said the preliminary loss is about ₹4,600 crore after an aerial survey of the Krishna and Godavari regions. He said preparedness checked loss of life, putting the toll at seven (earlier reports said five). At least 8,000 electric poles were uprooted in Konaseema alone. Compensation: ₹1,500 for each coconut and oil palm tree that fell; crop compensation will follow enumeration; relief-camp families get a 25-kg rice bag and cash. A Central team will visit Konaseema and West Godavari on Friday; he also hinted at exploring underground power cables in cyclone-prone areas.

  • Landfall: this report says the cyclone crossed the coast near Narsapur (West Godavari); earlier reports named Odalarevu and Razole (Konaseema). Both are on the same stretch of coast.
  • Allegation: YSR Congress Party (YSRCP) president Y.S. Jagan Mohan Reddy alleged the government understated crop losses, citing severe damage to “nearly 12 lakh acres” and unpaid Cyclone Montha input subsidy of ₹600 crore. These are an opposition party’s claims, not official findings.

📌 Static GK

After a Central team’s assessment, aid from the National Disaster Response Fund is considered by a high-level committee. Input subsidy is a payment to farmers for crop loss. Real Time Governance Society is the State’s data-monitoring body.

UPSC 🎯 Prelims pointers

New low-pressure area over the Andaman Sea may bring more rain from 1 November

GS-I GeographyWeather · Bay of BengalLow-pressure area · Depression · Rainfall deficitPrelims

The India Meteorological Department said a low-pressure area formed over the south Andaman Sea at 5:30 a.m. on 29 October. It is likely to move west-northwest, become a depression around 31 October and move towards the Andhra Pradesh coast by 2 November, bringing heavy rain on 1 and 2 November. An expert at the Cyclone Warning Centre, Visakhapatnam, said another cyclone is possible but it is not yet clear. Rayalaseema, which received little rain from Arnab, may again get none. Of 28 districts, 12 had normal rainfall and 16 were deficient; the rain cut the deficit in North Andhra.

📌 Static GK

A cyclonic storm needs winds of 62–88 km/h; a depression has 31–49 km/h. The northeast monsoon gives Andhra’s coast and Tamil Nadu most of their autumn rain.

UPSC 🎯 Prelims pointers

  • Depression; low-pressure area; Bay of Bengal.

APPSC / TGPSC 🏛️ Exam angle
