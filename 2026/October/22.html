Supreme Court stays probe into FIR against Pinarayi Vijayan in the CMRL-Exalogic case

GS-II PolityJudiciary · PMLA · Corruption casesSection 66(2) PMLA · ECIR · Vijay Madanlal ChaudharyMains

A Special Bench of Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V. Mohana, sitting in the Dasara vacation, stayed for seven days the police investigation into an First Information Report (FIR) against former Keralam Chief Minister Pinarayi Vijayan, his daughter T. Veena and P.A. Mohamed Riyas in the ₹2.78 crore CMRL-Exalogic payoff case. The allegations are the subject of the case and remain unproven.

  • Ruling: the court set aside the High Court Division Bench’s 15 October order and directed it to hear the appeals; Justice Bagchi said “we will not disturb the FIR, but we will stall the investigation”.
  • Law: the State relied on the 2022 Vijay Madanlal Chaudhary judgment, which treated the Enforcement Directorate’s (ED) passing of information under Section 66(2) of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA) as a direction to register an FIR. Justice Bagchi said States cannot treat judgments as statutes and that the Enforcement Directorate (ED) and Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA) “were not omniscient”.
  • ECIR: the court gave no opinion on the Enforcement Case Information Report and left the petitioners free to challenge it in the appropriate forum.
🎬 The CMRL-Exalogic case

📌 Static GK

The Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 is enforced by the Enforcement Directorate. An ECIR is the ED’s internal case record, equivalent to an FIR for money laundering. Article 142 lets the Supreme Court pass orders for complete justice.

UPSC 🎯 Prelims pointers

  • PMLA 2002; ECIR vs FIR.
  • Section 66(2) – information-sharing with other agencies.

APPSC / TGPSC 🏛️ Exam angle

India expected to mandate 1% sustainable aviation fuel blending on international flights from 2027

GS-III EnvironmentAviation · Climate · CORSIASAF · CORSIA · ICAOPrelims

Industry sources say India plans to require 1% blended Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) in international flights from 2027, 2% from 2028 and 5% from 2030. An official announcement is awaited, and a national SAF policy is not yet final.

  • CORSIA: the Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation, adopted by the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) in 2016, caps emissions from international flights at 85% of 2019 levels using SAF or eligible carbon credits. Its third, mandatory phase runs from 2027 to 2035.
  • Cost: Indian airlines face about 5 million tonnes of CO2 offsetting a year; at $10–15 per credit that is $50–75 million annually (SAF Association). SAF costs 2–5 times conventional jet fuel (ATF).
  • Supply: Indian Oil’s Panipat refinery is prepared for 30,000 tonnes of capacity by co-processing used cooking oil.
🎬 SAF blending targets
All values are percentages; India’s are proposed, not yet notified.

📌 Static GK

SAF is made from non-petroleum feedstocks such as used cooking oil. ICAO is the United Nations agency for civil aviation, headquartered in Montreal. India is also working on ethanol blending in petrol.

UPSC 🎯 Prelims pointers

  • CORSIA – ICAO; mandatory phase 2027–2035.

APPSC / TGPSC 🏛️ Exam angle

GS-III Internal SecurityCyber security · BankingSBI · CERT-In · Dark Web · Personally Identifiable InformationPrelims

The State Bank of India (SBI) told stock exchanges that of about 3,75,000 records on the Dark Web, 3,897 customer records contained personally identifiable information, five days after it was alerted by its threat-intelligence provider, the Defence Cyber Agency and CERT-In.

eMudhra leaks eight web security keys; government sites rely on its certificates

The Bengaluru-based certificate authority eMudhra said eight security keys were published to browser root stores because a flaw in its Key Generation Ceremony produced a second set of weak keys.
