Supreme Court seeks ECI report on Form 7 misuse; ECI says no false forms were accepted

GS-II PolityElection Commission · Electoral rollsForm 7 · Uttar Pradesh SIR · Compliance reportMains

The Election Commission of India (ECI) told a Bench headed by CJI Surya Kant that although it found “false” Form 7 applications for deletion of voters in the Uttar Pradesh Special Intensive Revision (SIR), none were accepted and corrective steps were taken promptly. Senior advocate Dama Seshadri Naidu appeared for the Election Commission of India (ECI). The petitioner, Barabanki Member of Parliament (MP) Tanuj Punia, argued through Salman Khurshid and Shariq Ahmed that the ECI’s claim needs judicial scrutiny and that the lack of correction in U.P. “emboldened” bulk filing in Karnataka and Telangana. Counsel said that in the Hata constituency 32 Form 7s were filed, two signed by a booth-level officer, and all targeted one community (the petitioner’s allegation). The court asked for a note and said: “Let the Election Commission (EC) look into these issues, take necessary action and file a compliance report.” The U.P. Special Intensive Revision (SIR) ended in April and over two crore voters were removed.

📌 Static GK

Form 7 is used for objection to an entry or deletion; Form 6 for new enrolment. A compliance report is a report to the court on action taken. The Hindu’s Delhi booth report was covered on 30 October.

UPSC 🎯 Prelims pointers

  • Form 7; Form 6; compliance report.

APPSC / TGPSC 🏛️ Exam angle

  • Tanuj Punia – Barabanki MP.

West Bengal SIR: ECI’s appeals against 16.10 lakh cleared voters raise legal questions

GS-II PolityElection Commission · Representation of the People ActAppellate Tribunals · Logical discrepancy · Statutory authorityMains

A news analysis says officials filed appeals before the Appellate Tribunals against the inclusion of about 16.10 lakh West Bengal voters whom judicial officers had cleared after they were flagged for “logical discrepancies”. Under the Representation of the People Act, only the Chief Electoral Officer, District Election Officers, Electoral Registration Officers and Assistant EROs are the authorities for electoral rolls. The Election Commission of India (ECI)’s affidavit of 26 October described the filers as “officers of the office of Chief Executive Officer (CEO) West Bengal” and said it learnt of the appeals on 22 October. Roughly 60 lakh “doubtful” cases were examined by about 700 judicial officers, who restored 32,77,360 voters and rejected 27.23 lakh, of whom about 22 lakh appealed. Election Commissioners S.S. Sandhu and Vivek Joshi had raised the appeals in file notings, per the report. Congress demanded cancellation; the Tribunals were created by the Supreme Court in 2026, a point the petitioners say the Act does not cover.

🎬 Adjudication of “doubtful” cases
Figures as reported in the analysis (lakh voters); some groups overlap.

📌 Static GK

The Representation of the People Act, 1950 governs preparation of electoral rolls. Article 142 lets the Supreme Court pass orders to do complete justice, the basis for such interim arrangements.

UPSC 🎯 Prelims pointers

  • Representation of the People Act (RP Act) 1950; ERO; Article 142.

APPSC / TGPSC 🏛️ Exam angle

  • Appellate Tribunal – created by Supreme Court (SC) order.

Six killed as a helicopter from Badrinath crashes near Joshimath

GS-III Disaster ManagementAviation safety · UttarakhandOrange alert · DGCA · AAIBPrelims

A helicopter operated by private firm Trans Bharat Aviation crashed at about 1:45 p.m. near Chai village in Joshimath (Chamoli district) while flying from Badrinath to Dehradun, killing all six aboard: pilot Captain Himanshu Sabharwal and five pilgrims from Karnataka. Preliminary reports suggest bad weather. The State grounded the operator’s entire fleet; officials of the Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) (Directorate General of Civil Aviation) and Aircraft Accident Investigation Bureau (AAIB) (Aircraft Accident Investigation Bureau) are to join the probe. Activist Anoop Nautiyal said flights continued despite an orange alert for five districts; Uttarakhand Civil Aviation Development Authority Chief Executive Officer (CEO) Prateek Jain said the weather was clear and four other sorties landed safely. Congress alleged negligence. In 2025 a series of helicopter crashes in the Char Dham route included six killed on 8 May and seven on 15 June.

GS-II GovernanceLabour · Social securityCode on Social Security · Gig workers · BoardsPrelims

GS-II PolityLocal government · ElectionsULB polls · Supreme Court deferment · Unanimous electionsPrelims

The State Election Commission is ready for elections to 100 urban local bodies, comprising 13 Municipal Corporations and 87 Municipalities and Nagar Panchayats, on 1 November. Naidu announces drain modernisation of ₹3,000 crore and a relief package

GS-III Disaster ManagementRelief · Irrigation drainsInput subsidy · Ex gratia · Drain modernisationPrelims

Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu said the Water Resources Department proposed about ₹3,000 crore to modernise irrigation drains in the Godavari region, choked at confluences, to end crop inundation. He confirmed ₹5 lakh ex gratia per death and ₹25,000 per hectare input subsidy for damaged crops; he added ₹18,000 per hectare for aquaculture ponds, ₹10,000 per damaged house, ₹25,000 for handloom equipment and ₹9,000–25,000 for damaged boats. He said power for homes would be restored by Sunday and four more days were needed for farm supply. Percentages derived from the counts reported; the three figures are not additive.

UN inquiry says Israeli forces continued war crimes after the Gaza truce

The Independent International Commission of Inquiry on the Occupied Palestinian Territory, chaired by Justice Srinivasan Muralidhar, told the United Nations (UN) General Assembly that Israeli military operations and settler violence in the West Bank were a coordinated attack to entrench settlements and displace people. It said over 75,000 Palestinians, including at least 21,500 children, had been killed since 7 October 2023 and that the toll kept rising after the October 2025 ceasefire; 34,176 West Bank residents remained displaced in August 2026. It called on Israel to end its occupation in line with the International Court of Justice’s July 2024 advisory opinion. Israel’s UN envoy Danny Danon rejected the report as “works of historical fiction”. These are the commission’s findings and Israel’s rebuttal; Israel denied the commission access.

📌 Static GK

The International Court of Justice is the UN’s principal judicial organ and gives advisory opinions that are not binding; the International Criminal Court tries individuals. Commissions of inquiry are set up by the UN Human Rights Council.

India slashes oil imports from the U.S. and Russia in September as the import bill rises

GS-III EconomyEnergy · External sectorCrude imports · Russia Sanctions Act · Import billMains

India imported 209.4 lakh tonnes of crude in September 2026, 9.5% more than August and 4.1% more than a year earlier. The oil import bill was about $14.3 billion, 16.4% higher than August and 33% higher than September 2025, because of high prices. Russia and the United Arab Emirates (UAE) together supplied 60% of imports. Russia’s share peaked at about 51% in July 2026. The U.S. Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026, signed on 18 September, allows tariffs of up to 100% on countries such as India and China buying large quantities of Russian oil and gas; the tariffs are in abeyance after an executive order on 16 October. The report says India’s cuts appear to respond to the Act.

📌 Static GK

Crude oil is India’s largest import by value; about 85% of consumption is imported. A sanction is a penalty imposed by one country to change another’s behaviour; “secondary” tariffs target third-country buyers.

World Bank: India made “remarkable progress” in reducing poverty

GS-III EconomyPoverty · EmploymentPoverty line · MPI · Youth unemploymentMains

The World Bank’s October 2026 Poverty and Equity Brief says the share below the Lower Middle-Income line ($4.2 a day) fell from 57.7% in 2011 to 16.1% in 2023, lifting about 496 million people out of poverty; extreme poverty ($3.00) fell from 27.1% to 2.6%. On the multidimensional measure, poverty fell from 53.8% in 2005-06 to 16.4% in 2019-21 and 15.5% in 2022-23. Employment grew by about 134 million in 2017–2023, but youth unemployment rose from 14.6% to 15.9% between two recent quarters, real earnings were broadly unchanged and the top 10% of workers earned about 13 times the bottom 10%. A belt from Uttar Pradesh to West Bengal holds about 57% of India’s poor. The Finance Minister welcomed the findings.

🎬 Poverty headcount
World Bank measures at international poverty lines, not the Indian poverty line.

📌 Static GK

The Multidimensional Poverty Index measures health, education and living standards; OPHI is the Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative. International poverty lines are set in purchasing power parity terms.

NVS-03 launch · NavIC

GSLV-F18 places the NVS-03 navigation satellite into orbit

GS-III Science & TechnologySpace · NavigationNavIC · NVS-03 · Atomic clockPrelims

ISRO’s GSLV-F18 launched the 2,250 kg NVS-03 from the second launch pad at Sriharikota late on Friday, placing it in the intended geosynchronous transfer orbit 18 minutes later. It is the third next-generation navigation satellite for the NavIC (Navigation with Indian Constellation) system, with a 12-year mission life, an indigenous atomic clock and an added L1 signal beyond L5 and S. NavIC covers India and about 1,500 km around it. The launch is expected to restore a constellation weakened by repeated atomic clock failures; the last working clock on IRNSS-1F stopped on 13 March and the earlier replacement NVS-02 did not reach its intended orbit. It is only ISRO’s third launch of 2026, after PSLV-C62 on 12 January (a failure) and GSLV-F17 with EOS-05 on 4 September (a success).

📌 Static GK

NavIC is India’s regional navigation system (formerly IRNSS) with seven original satellites. A geosynchronous transfer orbit is an elliptical orbit from which a satellite reaches geostationary height. Atomic clocks give precise timing for position fixes.

  • NavIC; GTO; atomic clock.

APPSC / TGPSC 🏛️ Exam angle

Editorial: Beyond gullibility – bank measures can only limit damage from “digital arrest” scams

GS-III Internal SecurityCyber fraud · Banking regulationMule accounts · Zero-liability circular · Debit holdsMains

The editorial says the CJI’s exasperation is understandable, with about ₹800 crore lost this year, but argues victims succeed because they fear law enforcement and police are seen as high-handed. It welcomes the Reserve Bank of India (RBI)’s KYC Amendment Directions, 2026, which require banks to flag suspected mule accounts and put temporary debit holds, noting Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) investigations show mule accounts are central and some bank staff helped open them. It says the court’s call to compensate victims addresses a gap in the RBI’s 2017 zero-liability circular, which covers only unauthorised transactions. It cautions that the Directions allow any unusual transaction of ₹1,000 or more to be held for up to 60 days and that this could hurt small traders and Jan Dhan holders. These are the editorial’s arguments.

📌 Static GK

A mule account is a bank account used to receive and move fraud proceeds. The RBI’s 2017 circular limits customers’ liability for unauthorised electronic transactions; authorised-but-coerced transfers fall outside it.

UPSC 🎯 Prelims pointers

  • Mule account; zero-liability circular.

APPSC / TGPSC 🏛️ Exam angle

  • Debit hold – up to 60 days.

Opinion: How AI is transforming the U.S. military industrial complex

GS-III Defence TechnologyDefence · AISoftware-defined warfare · Neo-primes · Enterprise contractsMains

Colonel Shashank Ranjan argues the core of the U.S. military industrial complex is shifting from hardware makers to software and Artificial Intelligence (AI) companies. He cites the White House Accord on Super Intelligence signed on 29 September 2026 and May 2026 agreements with eight AI firms for classified networks. He says the Defence Autonomous Warfare Group’s budget projection jumped from $225.9 million in FY26 to $54.6 billion in financial year 2026-27 (FY27), and the Army’s 10-year $20 billion Anduril contract consolidated over 120 procurement actions.
