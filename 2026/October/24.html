Durga Puja pandal stampede in north Kolkata kills 19

GS-III Disaster ManagementCrowd safety · West BengalStampede · Traumatic asphyxia · Crowd managementMains

On 17 October (Saptami), a stampede at the Chorbagan Sorbojanin Durga Puja pandal in north Kolkata killed 19 people, with 25 injured, according to the police. Preliminary post-mortem reports attributed most deaths to traumatic asphyxia caused by chest compression. The paper’s ground report raises questions about accountability in West Bengal under the new government of Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari.

  • Residents’ allegation: a resident said there were no policemen and no security, and organisers made wrong announcements that spread panic. This is an allegation quoted in the report.
  • Lesson: narrow venues, poor lighting and a sudden rush are classic stampede triggers.

Disaster management in India is governed by the Disaster Management Act, 2005; the National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) has issued crowd-management guidelines for events.

Odisha puts all districts on alert as a cyclone is likely to cross the coast by 28 October

GS-I GeographyCyclones · Bay of BengalIMD · Red alert · Disaster response forcesPrelims

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) said the system is likely to cross the southern Odisha–northern Andhra Pradesh coast by 28 October. Government to redesign the Periodic Labour Force Survey to track gig and unpaid work

GS-III EconomyLabour statistics · PLFSPLFS · NSSO · Digital platform workersPrelims

The National Sample Survey Office (NSSO) under the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation proposes changes to the Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) from January 2027: a separate Second Stage Stratum to identify households with a digital platform worker, and questions on unpaid household, caregiving, volunteer and trainee work and on labour underutilisation. The PLFS gives the unemployment rate and labour-force participation. Labour underutilisation covers people who are unemployed or want more work than they get.

Draft rules raise the pass mark for the learner’s licence test from 60% to 75%

GS-II GovernanceTransport · Motor Vehicles RulesCentral Motor Vehicles Rules 1989 · Learner’s testPrelims

Draft amendments to the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989 propose a 75% pass mark for the preliminary learner’s test, 15 days instead of seven to complete the process, a safe-driving tutorial of at least 30 minutes, separate proofs of address, age and identity, and a new Form 2A combining renewal, duplicate and update applications. No fee is proposed for changing a name or address; applicants must also give more medical information.

📌 Static GK

The Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 is the parent law; the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989 frame the procedures. Road transport is on the Concurrent List.

UPSC 🎯 Prelims pointers

  • CMVR 1989; Form 2A.

APPSC / TGPSC 🏛️ Exam angle

  • Learner’s licence – pass mark proposed 75%.

Opinion: BJP has no bad option, Congress has no good option on delimitation

GS-II PolityDelimitation · Lok Sabha seatsDelimitation · 850 seats · CensusMains

The author, Varghese K. George, writes that in April 2026 the government said it would keep each State’s current proportion in an enlarged Lok Sabha of up to 850 seats. Implementing this would need a constitutional amendment. The Congress wants the present 543 seats and their distribution kept, which also needs an amendment. He notes the Constitution currently requires reallocation of seats on the basis of the census.

  • Women’s reservation: one-third of seats will be reserved after the next delimitation regardless of the total.
  • Opposition view: a larger House widens absolute differences between States even if proportions are kept; the DMK supports keeping proportions but wants clarity.

📌 Static GK

Delimitation is redrawing constituency boundaries. Article 81 caps the Lok Sabha at 550 members (530 from States, 20 from Union Territories); the 84th Amendment (2001) froze the allocation of seats among States until the first census after 2026.

UPSC 🎯 Prelims pointers

  • Delimitation; Article 81.

APPSC / TGPSC 🏛️ Exam angle

  • Lok Sabha – proposed up to 850 seats.
